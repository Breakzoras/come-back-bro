# Changelog

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-04)

New: coming back mode, for when it does not work out and you want out.
- Turn it on with "coming back mode" or "I'm out". It replaces intoxicated mode until "end coming back mode".
- Lock the verdict in your own words. Use a nickname for the person if you ask.
- Cut the feed: mute and archive the chat, no profiles or stories, one kind closing line if they write later.
- Shared places like work or a friend's shop: polite and neutral, the extra attention drops away, nothing that reads as hurt.
- Thoughts that come back: name them, switch to a task, expect waves. The "why it did not fit" list is a tool you choose.
- Worth reminders on request, tied to true facts about you, because generic affirmations can backfire with low self-esteem.
- Three new sources in `EVIDENCE.md`.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-02)

Rebuilt on research. Every rule now points to its sources in `come-back-bro/EVIDENCE.md`, full notes in `research/`.
- New: five rules above everything (AI disclosure, agree only with facts, the user writes their own messages, the other person's boundaries, safety over "say it once").
- New: "when the thoughts will not stop" (person time plus an if-then plan), a slow-reply routine, the liking gap after a date.
- New: ask clearly and take a no; boundary and stalking rules in both directions; romance scam signs.
- New: crisis protocol with helplines; concrete markers for suggesting a professional; breaks and a clean exit; a rule for users under 18.
- New: body and safety kit: tested breathing, sleep tools, driver drinks zero, alcohol and consent, spiking, general diabetes warnings with no numbers.
- Changed: the 20/40 second traffic light became "follow up, then a story" (no source existed for 20/40). The drink bet became a dessert or song bet. "Next day" became "next morning". The insecurities reminder became a pacing rule. Notes about the other person are now minimal.
- Changed: the ChatGPT instructions follow the same rules and fit the 8,000 character limit.
